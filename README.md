## ✨ Overview

**BillMaker** is a powerful and sleek dashboard frontend built specifically for small restaurants, food stalls, and cafés. Designed with simplicity and speed in mind, it helps manage your entire food business from a single place—without relying on expensive software.

Whether you're billing, tracking revenue, or managing kitchen orders, **BillMaker** is designed to scale with your needs.

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ Generate printable food bills instantly
- ✅ Real-time revenue tracking
- ✅ Complete finance and profit reports
- ✅ Inventory tracking & stock management
- ✅ Table booking and reservation management
- ✅ Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) system
- ✅ Order history with data persistence (backend-ready)
- ✅ Built for thermal printer integration
- ✅ Fully responsive & customizable using Tailwind CSS
